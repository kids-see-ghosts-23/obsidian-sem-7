# VR / AR — Map of Content

Subject hub for Virtual Reality and Augmented Reality (IS372IA). The CIE-1 notes build from Unit I and the start of Unit II; the CIE-2 notes continue Unit II from Prefabs and cover Unit III.

## CIE-1

- [[VRAR CIE-1 Notes]] — Unit I (VR/AR/MR definitions, hardware, software, human perception, history) and Unit II (Unity basics, scenes, code blocks, debugging, objects, scripts), with a PYQ-to-section map.
- [[VRAR PYQ CIE-1]] — every question from the two real papers solved, plus a quiz-question bank.
- [[vrar]] — raw lecture notes and quick keyword lists (four elements, immersion, types of VR, VRML, Unity basics).
- [[sample progs]] — three ready-to-attach Unity C# scripts: mouse rotation, continuous rotation, smooth movement.
- [[pyq cie 1]] — earlier quiz/PYQ answers, including VR vs AR, the three I's, input devices and Stevens' power law.

## CIE-2
