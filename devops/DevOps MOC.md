# DevOps — Map of Content

Subject hub for DevOps. Start with the quick revision, then drill into the unit notes; the lab answers are the applied/practical side.

## Core notes

- [[devops-quick-revision]] — one-page revision: cloud vs bare metal, DevOps culture, containers, Kubernetes, scaling and cost.
- [[unit 3-4 quick recap]] — 10-minute recap of units 3 and 4 with the numbers to memorise.

- [[devops-lab-answers]] — lab questions answered: container builds, multi-stage Dockerfiles, Kubernetes deployments, pipelines and more.
