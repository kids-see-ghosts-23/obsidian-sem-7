# IKS — Map of Content

Subject hub for Indian Knowledge Systems (HS271TA). Two CIEs, each with a full-notes file and a solved-PYQ companion.

## CIE-1

- [[IKS_CIE1]] — Unit I (introduction, historicity, traditional knowledge, Vedic corpus, philosophy) and Unit II (linguistics, Panini, phonetics, number systems, zero, Pingala, knowledge framework).
- [[IKS_CIE1_PYQ]] — CIE-1 2025 paper solved, plus question-bank and MCQ practice.

## CIE-2

- [[IKS_CIE2]] — Unit II (number system onwards), Unit III (mathematics, astronomy, engineering, metallurgy, dyes, perfumes) and Unit IV (Yoga, Ayurveda, water management, town planning, Vastu, temples).

## Suggested reading order

1. [[IKS_CIE1]] → [[IKS_CIE1_PYQ]] — build the foundation and test it against the paper.
2. [[IKS_CIE2]] → [[IKS_CIE2_PYQ]] — continue into science, engineering and humanities.
